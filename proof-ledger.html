Methodology and boundaries
The server journal is research-only and has no order authority. Exact outcomes use the first Binance Spot 1-minute close at or after the target horizon when that exact resolver is available. The journal also stores a scheduled-observation outcome method; this Proof surface prefers exact_outcomes where present. Historical evidence shown here comes from each journal record's own why, against, invalidation, source and captured values. Decision Assurance is a separate current-state evidence system and is not retroactively attached to historical observations.
Proof is deliberately uncomfortable: good calls, bad calls, immature samples and missing resolutions all remain part of the record.
