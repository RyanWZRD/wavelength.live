Decision boundary
What this suite is and is not allowed to do
No execution authority. Scores are not probabilities, research weights are not orders, observational attribution is not causal evidence, and no page in this suite may mutate a frozen strategy. Missing evidence stays missing rather than being silently filled with a neutral value.
Wavelength Decision Intelligence is a read-only research system. It does not authorise trades, live capital, strategy promotion or automatic parameter changes.
