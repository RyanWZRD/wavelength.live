Ask the research system directly.

Ask about an asset, compare two theses, inspect what changed, test a candidate against your portfolio, or interrogate Wavelength's historical proof. Answers are assembled only from Wavelength's published evidence stores.

Ask Wavelength is not a free-form market oracle. If the required evidence is absent, incompatible or immature, the answer says so. It does not convert research scores into probabilities or invent missing facts.

What Ask Wavelength can and cannot infer
Current thesis questions use Decision Assurance and Opportunity Radar. Historical proof uses the frozen server decision journal. Change questions use durable Intelligence History only when a like-for-like WAVELENGTH_INTELLIGENCE_V1 series is available. Portfolio questions use only holdings stored in this browser. No correlation benefit, tax impact, personal risk tolerance, future return or execution permission is inferred unless Wavelength has explicit evidence for it.
Ask Wavelength explains Wavelength's own research. It does not place orders and does not replace the underlying evidence surfaces.
