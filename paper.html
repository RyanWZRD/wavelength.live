What this report is for
Evidence before promotion

The goal is not to manufacture a profitable-looking week. The goal is to learn whether broad-market Wavelength selection has positive expectancy after realistic trading frictions.

Only after a meaningful forward sample across different regimes should these paper results influence any decision about real capital.
