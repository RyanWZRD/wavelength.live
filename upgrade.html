Why checkout is deliberately disabled

Wavelength is currently hosted as a static GitHub Pages application. A browser-only paywall would not securely protect paid research payloads. Before charging anyone, Wavelength will connect a real identity provider, server-verified subscription entitlements, payment webhooks/customer portal and an authenticated delivery path for Pro data.

No payment is taken on this preview page. Crypto research only; no personalised financial advice or order authority.
