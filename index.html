Why Wavelength is different

The moat is the decision record.

Evidence with provenance

Evidence Map exposes direct versus proxy evidence, provider concentration, freshness and missing families instead of hiding dependency risk inside one score.

Theses that can change

Thesis Watch remembers the reasoning fingerprint and surfaces material changes in support, objections, provider coverage and invalidation — not ordinary price noise.

A system that tries to disagree

Decision Room and Red Team make the strongest objection part of the product. Wavelength is designed to show why a high-priority idea may still be unconfirmed.
